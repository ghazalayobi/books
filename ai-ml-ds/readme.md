### Overview
*Naked Statistics* introduces statistical concepts through practical examples and real-world applications. It explores how statistics help us understand data, identify patterns, evaluate claims, and make better decisions.

### Key Concepts

- **Descriptive Statistics:** Understanding data through measures such as mean, median, and standard deviation.
- **Probability:** Quantifying uncertainty and understanding the likelihood of events.
- **Sampling:** Understanding how samples represent populations and why sampling methods matter.
- **Correlation vs. Causation:** Recognizing that relationships between variables do not necessarily imply causality.
- **Regression Analysis:** Understanding relationships between dependent and independent variables, interpreting coefficients, and evaluating model results.
- **Statistical Significance:** Using hypothesis testing, p-values, t-statistics, and confidence intervals to assess evidence.
- **R-squared:** Understanding how much variation in the outcome is explained by a regression model.

### Key Takeaways

1. Statistics are not just about calculations; they are about interpreting evidence.
2. Correlation does not establish causation.
3. Statistical significance does not necessarily imply practical significance.
4. Every statistical model has assumptions and limitations.
5. Understanding uncertainty is essential when making decisions based on data.
